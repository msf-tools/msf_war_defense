Privacy Policy

Effective August 30, 2026

MSF War Defense is a community analysis tool. It displays aggregate Marvel Strike Force Alliance War statistics and public game-character information. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Scopely.

Information this site handles

This site does not ask for an account, player ID, alliance membership, contact information, or payment information. It does not use cookies or first-party analytics and does not intentionally collect, store, or share personal information.

Game data

Character information is provided by Scopely through the documented MSF Developer API. Aggregate defensive-team statistics come from a provisional service used by the official MSF website. The data is provided to visitors on an “as is” basis and may be incomplete, delayed, changed, or unavailable.

Hosting and external services

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request information, such as an IP address and browser details, under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Character portraits may be loaded from Scopely-controlled asset servers, which may receive ordinary web-request information.

Changes

This policy will be updated if the site begins handling personal information or adds services that materially change its privacy behavior.
